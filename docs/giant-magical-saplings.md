# Giant magical saplings

The mega, sacred and enchanted sacred variants are available for both greatwood and silverwood. Four original saplings craft a mega sapling of the same wood. Sacred and enchanted sacred variants can appear in dungeon and stronghold library chests; each chest has a 4.4% chance to receive one of the four variants. The enchanted variant has an item glint.

The mega variant has a height of 54 blocks. The sacred variant uses the sacred-spring parameters (72–108 blocks high), while the enchanted sacred variant uses the massive-tree envelope (up to 250 blocks high). Available build height can shorten the latter two, subject to minimum heights. The massive canopy samples fewer branches than the original to limit its cost. Silverwoods create original TC4 pure nodes based on tree height: floor(height × 3 / 10), giving 16 for a mega tree, 21–32 for a full-height sacred tree, and up to 75 for a full-height enchanted sacred tree. Nodes are placed across trunk columns where needed. Generated materials are the original TC4 logs and leaves. Trees that started growing before this change retain their original generation settings.

The tree shapes use the supplied MFR 1.7.10 generator as a reference. The mapping of sacred and enchanted variants follows their intended sizes, reversing the mapping in that source branch.

Growth works with ordinary or magic bone meal and proceeds in batches of at most 256 positions per tick per sapling. The tree keeps its progress across saves. The generator does not force-load chunks: it pauses at an unloaded position until that chunk and the sapling's chunk are loaded. The sapling chunk must remain within simulation distance while the target chunk is loaded. The largest tree can span more than 20 chunks in radius, so finishing it may require increasing both server view distance and simulation distance; with smaller settings it can remain paused. Trees stop at the build-height limit, leave the sapling intact if their central trunk is obstructed, and do not replace solid buildings or block entities.
