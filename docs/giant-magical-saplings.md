# Giant magical saplings

The mega, sacred and enchanted sacred variants are available for both greatwood and silverwood. Four original saplings craft a mega sapling of the same wood. Sacred and enchanted sacred variants can appear in dungeon and stronghold library chests; each chest has a 4.4% chance to receive one of the four variants. The enchanted variant has an item glint.

The three sizes are 54, 96 and 160 blocks tall, with branch reach up to 18, 52 and 140 blocks. Silverwoods create respectively 2, 5 and 12 original TC4 pure nodes in their trunks. Generated materials are the original TC4 logs and leaves.

Growth works with ordinary or magic bone meal and proceeds in batches of at most 256 positions per tick per sapling. The tree keeps its progress across saves. The generator does not force-load chunks: it pauses at an unloaded position until that chunk and the sapling's chunk are loaded. This can require a larger view distance or returning to the tree while it grows, especially for the largest size. Trees stop at the build-height limit, leave the sapling intact if their central trunk is obstructed, and do not replace solid buildings or block entities.
